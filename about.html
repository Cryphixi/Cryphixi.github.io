About / Allena Oglivie

Design starts with the person using it.

We should not conform to the tools meant to make our lives easier. The tools should conform to us.

Why I work this way

I learned computers on my grandfather's 1980s machines. The question I still come back to is: “What if my grandmother used this?” Games made that question feel concrete. Portal showed me how a system can teach through the experience itself.

At UC Berkeley, I study computer science and data science and use those skills to build and test interfaces. I care about accessibility, privacy-first AI, and equity for marginalized communities.
